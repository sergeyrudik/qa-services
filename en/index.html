05 / BEFORE WE START

Straight to the point.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the scope, the state of the project, and the access we get. After an initial review I propose a scope and an estimate. For an audit or a pilot we can fix a limited scope.

Can we start with a small task?

Yes. For example, triaging failing tests, automating one group of API scenarios, or an AI pilot that prepares checklists.

We already have a QA team. Is this a fit?

Yes. I can take on a specific part: the architecture of your automated tests, regression speed, CI, or introducing an AI assistant. The result is handed over to your team.

What happens to code and data when working with AI?

Before the pilot we agree on the allowed data, the model, and the environment. We choose what may go to external services and what must stay inside your infrastructure.

Can we arrange support after the project?

Yes, we discuss the format separately: evolving the tests, consultations for the team, or regular help with releases.
